# Cancellation and refund reporting

Portfolio composes source-owned facts as of the requested date. Sales supplies standing activated contract value, active sold units, and `cancelled_sales`: activated contracts whose `cancelled_at` UTC date is on or before `as_of`. An open, pending, or withdrawn cancellation without a completed effective cancellation is not counted. A contract cancelled after the observation date still contributes to that earlier standing position.

Collections supplies gross confirmed buyer receipts, approved `refund_due`, standing confirmed cash-out `refund_confirmed`, and canonical Collection Account `refund_outstanding`. The three refund figures are kept in their source currency. The outstanding liability is not a negative receivable, and neither refund due nor cash-out is subtracted from gross receipts. Confirmed refunds that remain standing after a cancellation withdrawal remain historical cash-out, even while the withdrawn liability ceases to be due; the usual due = paid + outstanding reconciliation applies to a standing approved liability, not an abandoned case with historical cash-out.

New live Portfolio responses and management captures use explicit `refund_due`, `refund_confirmed`, and `refund_outstanding` codes instead of the old ambiguous `refunds` code. This is an atomic internal live-client update. Existing version-one snapshots are immutable: the optional cancellation count remains absent (not zero) when it was not captured, and canonical hash verification omits this newly optional field for old documents. Historical `refunds` remains readable as confirmed cash-out. Comparisons align that legacy cash-out with `refund_confirmed`, while absent due/outstanding/count facts have no numerical delta. No database migration, backfill, historical snapshot rewrite, FX conversion, or new financial calculation is required.
